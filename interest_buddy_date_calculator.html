Your half-month rule: Completed calendar months are counted. If 1–14 extra days remain, add half a month (0.5). If 15 or more extra days remain, add one full month (1.0).

Annual compounding adds the accumulated interest to the principal after every 12 charged months. For any remaining charged months, interest is calculated on the latest principal. This is a calculator; agree the date-counting and interest terms with the other party.
